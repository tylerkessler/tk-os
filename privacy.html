TK OS Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

TK OS is a personal tool used only by its developer, Tyler Kessler, with his own Google account.

What it accesses

With the owner's authorization, TK OS reads and organizes email in the owner's own Gmail account (for example, applying labels).

How data is used and stored

Email data is processed on the owner's own computer. It is not sold, shared, or transferred to any third party, and it is not used for advertising. OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's machine.

Google API Services

TK OS's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
